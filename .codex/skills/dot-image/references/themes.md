# Dot Image Theme Profiles

Use the profile selected by the user. If no theme is stated, use `fantasy`. Every profile inherits the expressive modern pixel-art direction; themes change the setting and accent palette, not the cluster-based rendering or readable silhouettes.

## `fantasy` (default)

- Prompt direction: adventurous storybook fantasy, expressive characters and creatures, weathered equipment, mysterious ruins, and focused warm magical accents.
- Palette behavior: balance earthy amber highlights against deep navy, teal, or plum shadows.

## `sci-fi`

- Prompt direction: compact science-fiction equipment, eccentric robots, industrial surfaces, luminous displays, and playful technological details.
- Palette behavior: emphasize navy and cobalt shadows with cyan light and small coral or amber accents.

## `modern`

- Prompt direction: expressive contemporary characters, recognizable everyday objects, practical clothing, lively interiors, and humorous small details.
- Palette behavior: use balanced neutrals with clear cool shadows and focused warm accents.

## `cute`

- Prompt direction: extra-charming proportions, playful silhouettes, warm colors, readable expressions, and lighthearted details.
- Palette behavior: use brighter base colors without losing deep outline and occlusion shadows.

## `horror`

- Prompt direction: quirky gothic adventure, eccentric ghosts and monsters, decayed props, berry-red accents, theatrical shadows, and no gore.
- Palette behavior: use a compact navy, plum, charcoal, and berry palette with sparse cream highlights.

## Shared Restrictions

Every theme still forbids sprite blur, soft antialiasing, uncontrolled gradients, automatic dithering, random pixel noise, and subpixel-looking edges. Background-only atmospheric effects may use controlled tonal transitions when gameplay silhouettes remain crisp. Theme selection does not change the fixed output grid or verification requirements.
